Company profile

CHIROPE focuses on cross-border logistics from China into Europe and North America. The team helps cargo owners coordinate transport mode selection, supplier handoff, documentation, destination delivery and tracking.

Operating advantages

  • 300+ specialist team supporting booking, customs support and delivery coordination.
  • 300+ supplier partners supporting rate planning and service coverage.
  • One-stop coordination across ocean, air, rail, road, warehousing and FBA delivery.

Market scope

CHIROPE focuses on safe commercial routes across North America, Western and Northern Europe, Southern Europe, and selected Central and Eastern Europe destinations.

  • North America: United States and Canada.
  • Western and Northern Europe: United Kingdom, Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland and Sweden.
  • Southern Europe: Italy, Spain and Portugal.
  • Central and Eastern Europe: Poland, Czech Republic, Hungary and Romania.
