How to prepare cartons for FBA inbound shipping.

FBA shipments move faster when carton data, labels and delivery requirements are ready before cargo leaves origin.

FBApreparation checklist for inbound cargo teams.
Back to NewsRead time: 4 minutesView FBA solution

Most avoidable FBA delays start before the shipment reaches the destination. The cargo may be ready physically, but labels, carton counts, dimensions or appointment information may still be incomplete.

1. Confirm carton data early

Prepare carton count, gross weight, dimensions and SKU distribution before requesting a final quote. This helps CHIROPE compare transport modes and understand handling requirements.

2. Check labeling before pickup

Labels should be clear, scannable and placed according to destination requirements. If carton labels are missing or inconsistent, the issue can create extra handling after arrival.

3. Share appointment requirements

Marketplace delivery often depends on appointment rules. Share any delivery window, warehouse destination and booking data as soon as it is available.

Quote-ready checklist

  • Shipment ID or destination reference.
  • Carton count, weight and CBM.
  • SKU or product category notes.
  • Preferred mode and target arrival date.
  • Any pallet, labeling or appointment requirement.
